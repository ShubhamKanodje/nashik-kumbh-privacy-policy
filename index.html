Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Seven-Hub Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") provides the Nashik Kumbh 2027 mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or phone number to use its main features. The app does not request the user's current GPS location.

To deliver push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a device registration token and technical information required for notification delivery.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the app may be used to:

  • Deliver event updates and app notifications.
  • Display event information and details about places.
  • Maintain app functionality and service reliability.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps: Used to display map information and places. Google may process technical data when Maps features are used. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Used to deliver push notifications. Firebase Privacy Information
  • Supabase: Used to store and retrieve app content such as event and place information. Notification registration tokens may also be stored for notification delivery. Supabase Privacy Policy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party service providers listed above to operate app features and deliver notifications.

5. Data Retention and Notifications

Notification tokens may be retained while needed to deliver notifications and may be updated or removed when no longer valid. Users can disable app notifications through their device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us, please contact us.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through the app. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
